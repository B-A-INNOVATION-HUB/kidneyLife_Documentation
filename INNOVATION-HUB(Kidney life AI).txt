PART A: DOCUMENTATION ASPECTS
1.	KidneyLife AI’s Relevance to the Theme
The diagnosis of kidney disease depends on the timely reading of CT scans, yet in South Africa’s public hospitals it is often delayed by a shortage of radiologists and unreliable scanning capacity. Although the Fourth Industrial Revolution is widely promoted, few public-sector patients have felt its benefits. KidneyLife AI answers the theme “an AI Solution for Industries” by bringing deep learning into the healthcare industry as a decision-support tool for under-resourced hospitals such as Dr George Mukhari Academic Hospital in Ga-Rankuwa. The solution is trained on 12,446 labelled CT images from the publicly available CT Kidney Dataset and classifies each abdominal CT image as Normal, Cyst, Stone or Tumour, with a confidence score for every prediction. Scans about which the model is uncertain are flagged for radiologist review, so clinicians can attend to urgent patients first while the final diagnosis remains with the medical professional. In this way, the solution reduces diagnostic workload, shortens the time from scan to treatment, makes better use of existing CT equipment, and shows how affordable AI can serve communities that new technology usually leaves behind.
